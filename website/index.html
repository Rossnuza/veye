How it works

From tracker to peace of mind in three steps

1

Link the tracker

Scan the code on the back of the device, or type it in. No technical setup — it's paired in under a minute.

2

See the location

Open the app any time and see exactly where your child is on a live map, updated every 60 seconds.

3

Get instant alerts

Set up school, home, or grandma's house as safe zones. Get a notification the moment your child arrives or leaves.

The hardware

One tiny tracker. Worn and forgotten.

About the size of a matchbox. It runs on its own all day — no action needed from your child. GPS plus a standard SIM sends coordinates over the mobile network, even on 2G. If signal drops, it stores everything and sends it the moment signal returns.
