The hardware

One tiny tracker. Worn and forgotten.

About the size of a matchbox. It runs on its own all day — no action needed from your child. GPS plus a standard SIM sends coordinates over the mobile network, even on 2G. If signal drops, it stores everything and sends it the moment signal returns.
